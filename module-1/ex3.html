My Hobbies

Swimming

Swimming is my favorite hobby because it makes me feel like I’m flying underwater. I love the splash when I first jump in and how the water feels cool on my skin. When I dive down deep, everything gets quiet, and I can pretend I'm a fish or a dolphin. I practice different strokes like freestyle and backstroke to get faster, and it’s really fun to race my friends to the other side of the pool. Even though it's hard work and makes me tired, I always feel happy and refreshed after a long swim!

Cooking

really love cooking because it’s like being a scientist in the kitchen. It is so much fun to mix different ingredients together to see how they change into something delicious. My favorite part is adding spices and tasting the food as I go to make sure it’s just right. It feels awesome when I make a meal for my family and they tell me it tastes great! Even if I make a little bit of a mess, learning how to make my own snacks and dinners is the best part of my day.
